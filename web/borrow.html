Borrow to Earn Simulator

Strike's official scheme: lock collateral (e.g. ADA), receive USD credit at 0% interest, and the credit must go into the SLP vault. This simulator uses real data: the live ADA price from Strike's market API and historical SLP performance from the EarnScope tracker.

1. How it works

  1. Lock collateral, e.g. ADA worth C USD.
  2. Draw USD credit equal to the draw % of C, at 0% interest.
  3. The credit must be routed into the Strike Liquidity Provider (SLP) vault.
  4. Net result = credit value in SLP at the end of the horizon − credit principal.
  5. Line health = final collateral equity ÷ credit principal.

Strike does not publish an official maximum draw limit; 50% is used as a common example.
