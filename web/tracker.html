Position Tracker

Paste your Cardano address to see your positions across all Strike vaults. No login, no keys, purely from public data.

Cardano address

The address is normalized automatically (whitespace stripped, lowercased). Use a payment address (addr1...), not a stake address (stake1...).
Honest limitations: position data comes from Strike's public depositors snapshots (refreshed hourly by EarnScope). Per-account Borrow to Earn lines and official staking status CANNOT be tracked publicly because they sit behind Strike authentication. The on-chain STRIKE balance is fetched directly from Koios and may fail to load when the Koios network is slow.
Source: Strike public API (api.strikefinance.org) + Koios (api.koios.rest) · Built for the Strike 48h Hackathon
